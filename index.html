About

I'm Sarah, and I'm in my final year of a Data Science degree at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Badr University in Assiut. I came to this field from the hardware side — my first real training was forty hours of Arduino, sensors and embedded systems, wiring things up and watching them respond. What pulled me toward AI was the same thing, one level up: a machine taking in the world and reacting to it.

Most of my work since then has been in computer vision, because that's where the feedback is immediate — you point a camera at something and you can see straight away whether the model understood it or not. Alongside that I've built classification and regression models, trained small language models, and spent a fair amount of time on the unglamorous half of the work: cleaning data, engineering features, and checking that a good accuracy number isn't hiding something.

I also co-lead the AI Society at my university, where I run workshops and sit with students who are stuck on their first model. Teaching something is the fastest way to find the holes in your own understanding, and it's become my favourite part of the week. Right now I'm working through the Microsoft Machine Learning track at DEPI and looking for an internship where I can build alongside people who know more than I do.
